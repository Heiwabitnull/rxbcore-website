PRIVACY POLICY

RXB (Record X-core Blockchain) is a decentralized, open-source protocol designed with a "privacy-by-default" philosophy.

1. Data Collection

This website and the RXB protocol do not collect, store, or process any personal identification information (PII). We do not use analytical scripts, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies.

2. Network Layer (Tor)

RXB operates as a Tor-only network. When accessing the network through Tor, your IP address remains hidden from other nodes and the infrastructure. We encourage all users to maintain this layer of anonymity.

3. Essential Cookies

We only use essential session bits required for the basic functionality of the interface. These are stored locally on your device and are not used for user profiling.

4. On-chain Transparency

Please note that all transactions on the RXB blockchain are public. While addresses are pseudonymous, transaction history is permanently recorded on the ledger for evidentiary archiving purposes.
